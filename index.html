Numerical and Experimental modeling

Exploiting Bistability and Viscoelasticity in Reservoir Computing

Investigating how viscoelasticity can introduce short-term memory to address the critical memory–nonlinearity trade-off that governs performance.

This study combines numerics and experiment, devising a reservoir computer composed of bistable oscillators coupled to viscoelastic standard linear solid elements. Bistability introduces nonlinearity while viscoelasticity introduces short-term memory. We demonstrate that by tuning these parameters, the performance of the reservoir computer can be optimized.

Role: Lead researcher
Methods: Analytical modeling, time-integration, parameter studies
Tools: MATLAB, electronics

Numerical and Experimental Modeling

The Tymbal of a Cicada: Nature's Sound Generating Metastructure

Devising a novel metamaterial-inspired mechanical model that can mimic sound generation across different species of cicadas.

Set up coupled numerical and experimental studies to understand how tymbal buckling and abdominal resonance interact to produce loud, tonal acoustic output. Used the model to explore how geometry, material properties, and coupling affect sound generation and efficiency.

Role: Lead researcher
Methods: Nonlinear dynamics, experimental validation, reduced-order modeling
Tools: MATLAB, high-speed imaging, laser vibrometry, audio analysis

About Pritam

I am a PhD candidate in Mechanical Engineering, with a focus on understanding and improving the behavior of complex mechanical systems. My work typically combines analytical modeling, numerical simulation, and carefully designed experiments.

Before starting my PhD, I built a foundation in core mechanical engineering topics such as solid mechanics, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, and control. Over time, I've gravitated toward problems where high-fidelity models and real-world data need to work together—whether that means validating simulations in the lab or using experiments to refine assumptions.

Looking ahead, I'm interested in roles where I can continue to apply rigorous engineering thinking to practical problems, collaborate with multidisciplinary teams, and communicate complex results in a way that informs design and decision-making.
