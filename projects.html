Research projects

Everything I have worked on.

Most of my doctoral work asks a version of the same question: what happens when a structure that can snap between two states also remembers its recent past? The answer turned out to be useful for computation, for vibration control, for suppressing noise in a driveline, and — unexpectedly — for explaining how a cicada sings. Each project below has its own page with schematics and a live simulation.

Bio-inspired: the cicada tymbal as a metastructure
Thread 03
Nature Communications · 2025 · vol. 16
Role: Lead author
Methods: Reduced-order modeling · nonlinear dynamics · high-speed imaging · laser vibrometry · X-ray microscopy
With: R. Singh, H. Tao, E. Ganju, N. Chawla, J. M. Gibert, A. K. Bajaj

Cicadas produce extraordinarily loud, tonal calls through the interplay of tymbal buckling and body resonance, but no framework explained why calls differ so much between species. We reconceptualised the organ as a naturally occurring biological metastructure — periodically arranged ribs with inherent frequency filtering — and modelled it as a chain of bistable oscillators coupled to a chain of resonators. The model captures both the snap-through dynamics of the ribs and the acoustic filtering that follows, and is the first species-adaptable framework able to reproduce the distinct song frequencies and call patterns observed across multiple species.

Bistability and viscoelasticity in reservoir computing
Thread 01
Nonlinear Dynamics · 2025
Role: Lead author
Methods: Analytical modeling · time integration · Melnikov analysis · information-rate and memory-capacity metrics
With: J. M. Gibert, A. K. Bajaj

In electrical reservoir computers, short-term memory comes free from a delay line. In mechanical ones there is no such shortcut — viscoelasticity is the only mechanism by which a structure's state can depend on its deformation history. This project studies a viscoelastic bistable chain as a physical reservoir, using a bistable oscillator coupled to a standard linear solid element as the unit cell. Bistability supplies nonlinearity, viscoelasticity supplies fading memory, and the Deborah number sets the balance between them. Simulation identified the parameter windows where information rate and memory capacity peak, and a Melnikov-based criterion characterises the transition from useful dynamics into chaos.

Dynamics of the viscoelastic bistable von Mises truss
Thread 02
Int. J. Non-Linear Mechanics 2024 · J. Comput. Nonlinear Dynamics 2024 & 2025 · ICNDA
Role: Lead author
Methods: Harmonic balance · stability & continuation analysis · chaos diagnostics · high-frequency averaging
With: J. M. Gibert, A. K. Bajaj, Q. Zhao

The foundational thread of my PhD, spanning four papers. A lumped-parameter viscoelastic von Mises truss is governed by a second-order Duffing-type equation with quadratic nonlinearity coupled to a first-order equation for the viscoelastic force — together a third-order jerk system whose slow and fast timescales interact in genuinely surprising ways. The work establishes that viscoelasticity introduces additional timescales and degrees of freedom, that multiple coexisting periodic solutions arise, that an equivalent viscous approximation fundamentally fails to reproduce the dynamics, and that the system becomes chaotic under harmonic forcing. It also identifies the configuration that maximises energy dissipation, which matters directly for vibration suppression, and examines how high-frequency base excitation biases the system toward one equilibrium.

Robust control of stick-slip instability in automotive clutches
Patent pending
U.S. Patent Application No. 63/989902
Role: Lead inventor
Methods: Friction-induced vibration analysis · stability analysis · robust control design
Tools: MATLAB · Simulink

Clutch squawk is a self-excited vibration caused by a falling friction characteristic, which gives the steady-sliding equilibrium negative damping and drives it onto a stick-slip limit cycle. Rather than treating it with friction modifiers or broadband damping, I developed a control algorithm that injects damping into the unstable mode itself and holds up under substantial uncertainty in the friction law — which varies with temperature, wear and contamination.

Bio-inspired mechanical metamaterials for vibration and wave control
Thread 04
Doctoral research · design, characterisation, validation
Role: Design, fabrication and experimental validation
Methods: Dispersion analysis · band-gap design · laser vibrometry · material characterisation
Tools: FEA · silicone casting · 3D printing

Locally resonant lattices whose vibration behaviour comes from architecture rather than composition. Because the band gap is set by an internal resonance rather than the lattice spacing, attenuation is achievable at wavelengths far longer than the cell itself — the sub-wavelength behaviour that makes metamaterials practical at low frequency. The same physics read in reverse concentrates energy into instrumented cells for harvesting.

Analog hardware for nonlinear computing and structural health monitoring
Thread 05
FPAA and PCB systems · hardware machine learning
Role: Circuit design, fabrication and testing
Methods: Analog computing · physical reservoir computing · classification
Tools: FPAA · KiCad · PCB fabrication

If a reservoir's value is that its own physics performs the nonlinear transformation for free, implementing it digitally gives away the entire advantage. These circuits obey the governing equation continuously rather than solving it in steps, giving microwatt-scale sensor nodes that classify structural condition in real time instead of streaming raw data for offline analysis.

Vehicle–trailer dynamics and regenerative suspension
Thread 06
Doctoral research · modeling and simulation
Role: Modeling and simulation
Methods: Multi-body dynamics · structural vibration · energy-transfer analysis
Tools: Abaqus · Simulink · MATLAB

A hitch couples two sprung masses that would otherwise be analysed separately, creating modes belonging to neither vehicle alone and transferring energy in both directions. The work quantifies how much energy is genuinely recoverable at each axle over realistic road spectra, and how much damping can be diverted to harvesting before ride and stability degrade.

HVAC system design for a three-screen multiplex
Competition
ISHRAE National Student Design Competition
Scope: Load calculation, duct design, CFD verification
Tools: SolidWorks · CFD

Cinemas are a deceptively hard load case: extremely dense but intermittent occupancy, tall raked volumes that encourage stratification, and concentrated projection heat. Loads were computed per auditorium, the duct network laid out and sized in SolidWorks, and CFD used to verify that air actually reached the seating rather than short-circuiting to the return.
